# What squeegee measured

This file is generated by `research/analyze.mjs`. Rerun it after a new capture to refresh every number.

## Highlights

- In this sample, a cluster of stock defaults marks an AI-built page better than any single visual style. 65% of AI-built landing pages carry 5 or more of the nine stock defaults. 2% of the reference landing pages do.
- The strongest single signals are "shadcn-style theme variables present" (6% against 87%), "Three or more checkmark list items" (1% against 24%), "Lucide icons" (10% against 87%).
- 14 signals differed little between the groups, including "Dark page", "transition: all", "Centered headline", "No reduced-motion rule", "Large gradient area".
- 3 signals were more common on the reference pages: "Pure white page", "Blue-violet is 30% or more of colored area", "Geist".

## The sample

- **Reference pages:** 565 professionally made sites that loaded and were not blocked. By surface: landing 336, web app 107, mobile 64, desktop 58. The mobile and desktop rows are the product websites of mobile and desktop apps, not screens of the apps.
- **AI-built pages:** 273 public pages hosted on the publish domains of AI site builders. By builder: replit 88, lovable 79, base44 57, bolt 48, v0 1.
- **Landing-page comparison:** 282 reference landing pages against 84 AI-built pages. Both groups are limited to pages with 250 words or more that are at least 3 screens long.

The full list of reference sites is in [reference-sites.csv](reference-sites.csv). The AI-built pages are not listed: they are mostly individual people's projects, and this research is about patterns, not about them.

## Method

1. `capture.mjs` loads each URL in headless Chrome at 1440 px wide and reads computed styles from the rendered page: fonts, sizes, weights, colors, radius, shadows, gradients, transitions, layout patterns and text.
2. Pages behind a bot challenge, pages with a password field, and pages with fewer than 15 words are dropped.
3. `analyze.mjs` turns the measurements into yes or no signals per page and counts how many pages in each group show each signal.

No model reads the pages. The numbers below are counts.

## Limits

- These are associations in one sample, not proof of cause. No uncertainty ranges are computed, so treat differences of a few points as noise.
- A signal records that a pattern is present, not how well it is executed. A three-card row on a well-made site and on a generated page count the same.
- Rates are per page and are not adjusted for page length. The reference pages are several times longer than the AI-built ones, so they have more chances to contain any given pattern.
- Signals are measured on the rendered page and are broader than their plain names suggest. "shadcn-style theme variables present" means at least three of `--radius`, `--primary`, `--muted-foreground` and `--ring` are set on the root element. It does not check their values. "Lucide icons" and "Inter" mean any use. "No chosen font" means the most-used body family is a system face.
- The page is not scrolled, so content that loads on scroll is not measured. A reduced-motion rule inside a stylesheet served from another origin cannot be read and counts as absent.
- Fonts are the declared families, which may differ from the face that loaded. Contrast ignores background images.
- "AI-built" means hosted on a builder's publish domain. That does not show how much manual work a page received. The pages were found through Hacker News posts and a public scan index, so they are not a random sample of what the builders produce.
- The reference set leans toward established companies with design teams.
- Pages behind a bot challenge are dropped by title, which can miss some. Pages with a password field are dropped, which removes some legitimate pages. Captures that timed out before the network went idle are kept.
- Pages made with tools that have no publish domain of their own are missing.
